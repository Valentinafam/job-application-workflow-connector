## Cover Letter Pattern

Use 220-300 words unless the platform gives a stricter limit.

Structure:

1. Short opening and role interest.
2. Candidate background aligned to the role family.
3. Two or three skills or achievements matched to the job description.
4. Close with availability and interest in discussing fit.
