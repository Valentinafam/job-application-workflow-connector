## Hand Off to User

Stop and ask when:

- Legal, identity, work authorization, sponsorship, or compensation wording is unclear.
- CAPTCHA, anti-bot checks, login, or 2FA appears.
- Resume upload cannot be verified.
- Portfolio, video, writing sample, references, or custom materials are required.
- The final submission requires user review under Precision mode.

## Status Classification

- `Pending`: worth later review or application.
- `Submitted`: explicit confirmation evidence was observed.
- `Skipped`: intentionally skipped, with reason.
- `Blocked`: attempted but could not safely proceed.
- `Needs user`: missing user-owned fact or action blocks progress.
