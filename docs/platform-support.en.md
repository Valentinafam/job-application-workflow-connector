# Platform Support

## Best First Platform

LinkedIn Easy Apply is usually the best first trial because the form flow is comparatively short and easy to verify.

## Other Platforms

The workflow can be extended to:

- SEEK
- Glassdoor
- Greenhouse
- Lever
- Ashby
- Workday
- company career sites and ATS pages

Support depends on the actual page. Some sites have multi-step custom forms, account requirements, browser permissions, CAPTCHA, or anti-bot checks. Those should be treated as handoff points.

## Recommended Rollout

1. Lead-finding-only trial.
2. LinkedIn Easy Apply test with a small number of low-risk jobs.
3. Add one extra platform at a time.
4. Record blockers and repeated form answers in the dashboard.
5. Promote high-fit or government jobs to Precision mode.
